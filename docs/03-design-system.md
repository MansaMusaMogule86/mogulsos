# Design System

Core: Obsidian #08090D; Gold #E9D2A0; Ivory #F3F0E8.
Divisions: Digital #00DEFF; AI #A855F7; Growth #FF682C; Creative #FF4F92; Strategy #20BFA6.

Typography: choose licensed display and readable body fonts after visual review.
Layout: asymmetry, purposeful negative space, editorial hierarchy, purposeful content density.
UI elements: considered light lines, image masks, transitions that explain navigation.
Do not replicate gaming/esports UI conventions in B2B service areas.
Brand rule: preserve approved logo proportions and original colors; division colors illuminate scenes,
not recolor the official logo.

Accessibility: 4.5:1 body contrast, keyboard nav, visible focus, semantic heading structure,
minimum 44px primary touch targets and meaningful motion alternatives.
