# Complete Sitemap and Route Architecture

`/` redirects to `/en/`.
Only `/en/` is enabled until reviewed translations are complete.

- `/[locale]/` Home, hero, divisions, work, proof, about teaser, CTA.
- `/[locale]/services/` Overview of five divisions.
- `/[locale]/services/[division]/` Division landing page.
- `/[locale]/services/[division]/[service]/` Dedicated service landing page (data driven).
- `/[locale]/solutions/` and `/[locale]/solutions/[slug]/` Pre-packaged cross-division business outcomes.
- `/[locale]/industries/` and `/[locale]/industries/[slug]/` Sector pages.
- `/[locale]/work/` and `/[locale]/work/[slug]/` Approved projects only.
- `/[locale]/about/` Agency story, positioning, philosophy.
- `/[locale]/our-universe/` Immersive division explorer.
- `/[locale]/moguls/` Historic inspiration and operating principles.
- `/[locale]/process/` Discovery to post-launch methodology.
- `/[locale]/insights/` and `/[locale]/insights/[slug]/` Editorial library.
- `/[locale]/resources/` Tools, guides, PDFs, educational content.
- `/[locale]/faq/` Frequently asked questions.
- `/[locale]/careers/` Hiring/collaboration.
- `/[locale]/contact/` Contact channels.
- `/[locale]/start-a-project/` Project briefing, qualification form.
- `/[locale]/legal/privacy/`, `/terms/`, `/cookies/`, `/accessibility/`.
- `/api/health`, `/api/leads`, `/api/contact`, `/api/newsletter`, `/api/booking`, `/api/ai/concierge`.

Each division landing page should contain an intro, services, methodology, industry relevance,
case study proof if approved, FAQs, internal links, and project CTA.
Each service landing page should have: problem, audience, outcomes, deliverables, process,
integrations, differentiators, case proof, FAQs, related services and CTA.
