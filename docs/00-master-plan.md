# Master plan

Positioning: Mansas Moguls is the umbrella studio for five integrated practices.
Primary conversion: qualified consultation or project inquiry routed into GoHighLevel.
Audience: businesses, founders, operators and enterprises across UAE and international markets.
Aesthetic: preserve existing Mansas Moguls logo. Obsidian and gold brand foundation,
with controlled division colors and immersive interaction inspired by the **principles** of Active Theory,
not a copy of its work. No unlicensed photos, fonts or 3D assets.

## Technical layers
1. Document content, SEO metadata and static UI in Astro.
2. Isolate animation/3D as hydrated React islands with lifecycle cleanup.
3. Implement chapter-based, scroll-scrubbed GSAP orchestration, synchronized with a 3D camera timeline.
4. Build alternative static hero and content flow for low-powered/mobile/reduced-motion devices.
5. Route leads through secure server endpoints to GoHighLevel after explicit integration setup.
6. Keep MDX editorial content separate from code; enable a CMS only if real editorial requirements justify it.
7. No database needed for v1 marketing site; add a database only when workflows need one.

## Development gates
G0 Confirm sitemap, copy outline, scene storyboard and service ownership.
G1 Build design tokens and an accessible static skeleton.
G2 Prototype one hero 3D scene, scroll timeline and mobile fallback.
G3 Benchmark and approve performance.
G4 Expand to five division worlds; complete page templates and editorial system.
G5 Integrate CRM/forms/analytics, translations, SEO and legal requirements.
G6 QA, staging, security checks, content signoff and launch.
